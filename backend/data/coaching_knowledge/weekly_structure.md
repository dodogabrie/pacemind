# Weekly Training Structure

## Key Principles
- Distribute workouts to allow adequate recovery between hard sessions
- One long aerobic workout per week = 30-40% of weekly volume
- Separate high-intensity (Z3, Z4) sessions by at least 72 hours
- ME workouts also need 72-hour separation from Z3/Z4
- Recovery workouts fill gaps between hard sessions
- Strength and core workouts intersperse with aerobic work

## Week Types

### B (Base) Week
**Purpose:** Build aerobic capacity, running economy, strength
**Structure:**
- 1 long aerobic workout (30-40% of weekly distance/vertical) at Z1 or Z2
- 2 strength sessions (general or specific depending on needs)
- 2 core strength workouts
- Remaining volume: Z1-Z2 aerobic base
- 2 runs with fast downhill pick-ups
- Active recovery work 3x/week minimum (stretch, roll, massage, swim)
- If ADS: nearly all aerobic work in Z2; otherwise Z2 <15% of volume, rest in Z1

### I (Intensity) Week
**Purpose:** Introduce Z3-Z4 training while maintaining base
**Structure:**
- All of B Week structure, plus:
- 1 Hill Sprint/Bounding workout
- 1-2 Z3 interval sessions (starting at 5% of weekly volume, building to 10%)
- When Z3 comfortable, replace some with Z4
- Drop ME training as Z3/Z4 increases
- Continue 2 core workouts/week
- Z3/Z4 sessions separated by 72+ hours
- If ADS: nearly all base work in Z2; otherwise shift to Z1

### R (Recovery) Week
**Purpose:** Consolidation — allow body to catch up
**Structure:**
- Cut total volume to 50% of last big week
- At least 2 Recovery Zone workouts
- 1 Core Strength workout
- Hill Sprints/Bounding cut to 50% volume, late in week
- Cut all high-intensity work by half, 1 workout late in week
- Drop or cut ME by 50%
- Long workout (~40% of reduced weekly volume) late in week when recovered
- Focus on active recovery: stretch, roll, massage, swim, cross-training
- Should feel like attacking next week by Sunday — if not, didn't recover enough

### S (Specificity) Week
**Purpose:** Event-specific preparation in later weeks
**For ultra runners:**
- Back-to-back long runs (60-80% of weekly volume across 2 days)
- Only 1 Z4 session, separated by 72+ hours from long runs
- Cut all Z3; reduce ME

**For short races (VK, skimo):**
- Reduce aerobic base; double Z4 sessions (72+ hours apart)
- Z4 total time ≤15% of previous week
- Cut all Z3

### T (Taper) Week
**Purpose:** Gradually reduce load to arrive fresh without losing fitness
**Structure:**
- Reduce overall volume each week
- Cut intensity volume ~30%/week
- 1 Core Strength workout
- Hill Sprints cut 30%/week
- Long workout ~50% of weekly volume
- 1 Z2 workout ≤10% of weekly volume
- Keep intensity short with long rests (sharpening only)
- All other sessions: Recovery Zone
- Include a few short fast downhill runs/pick-ups
- Nothing new this week — won't make you faster, might make you slower

### G (Goal/Race) Week
**Structure:**
- No strength training
- Recovery Zone workouts only, max 4 days
- Spice 2 workouts with 3-4 x 30-sec accelerations at fun-fast pace, 3 min easy between
- Active recovery focus: stretch, roll, massage, swim
- Rest and relax but stay active daily to avoid sluggishness

## Modulation Patterns
- **Standard:** 3 build weeks : 1 recovery week (3:1)
- **Conservative:** 2 build : 1 recovery (2:1) — for newer athletes or poor recovery
- **Aggressive:** Planned overreaching block followed by extended recovery (advanced only)
- Recovery weeks: drop load 40-60% of last big week
- After overreaching: drop up to 70%

| Day | Session |
|-----|---------|
| Mon | Rest or Recovery Zone |
| Tue | Z1-Z2 aerobic + Core Strength |
| Wed | Z1-Z2 aerobic with pick-ups + General Strength |
| Thu | Recovery workout or rest |
| Fri | Z1-Z2 aerobic + Core Strength |
| Sat | Long Z1-Z2 aerobic (30-40% of weekly volume) |

|-----|---------|

| Sun | Z1-Z2 aerobic or Recovery |

## Race Season Management

### Between Races (2-3 weeks gap)
- Use gap for capacity-building training
- First few days: recovery from previous race
- Middle: base training focus (Z1-Z2 volume)
- Last few days: lighter training before next race
- If gap allows only 10 real training days, prioritize base over intensity

### Back-to-Back A Race Weekends
- No capacity-building possible in inter-race week
- Maintenance work only: 1 high-tempo workout + 1 aerobic maintenance session
- Rest should be recovery or technique work
- Nothing will make you faster; many things will make you slower

### B/C Race as A-Race Preparation
- Treat B/C race as hard workout within training week
- Light day before and after the race
- Use for testing strategy, pacing, equipment, nutrition
- Low-pressure opportunity for mental training

## Decision Rules for the Agent
- IF generating a training week THEN ensure hard sessions (Z3, Z4, ME) are separated by at least 72 hours
- IF athlete is in Base Period THEN use B or I week structure depending on AeT/LT spread
- IF athlete has <3 weeks to race THEN shift to T/G week structure
- IF athlete feels "off" after warmup THEN recommend converting to Recovery workout (not forcing planned session)
- IF athlete grades 2+ workouts C or worse in a week THEN next week should be Recovery week
- IF athlete can't maintain Z1-Z2 volume while adding intensity THEN reduce intensity volume until base is sustainable
- IF ultra race preparation THEN include back-to-back long days in S weeks (60-80% of weekly volume across 2 days)
- IF short race (VK) preparation THEN double Z4 sessions in S weeks with 72-hour separation
